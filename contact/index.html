Contact Us

The published way to contact CrakkerXworld.AI support.

How to contact support

Use the Feedback & Bug Reports form to send a message to CrakkerXworld.AI. Choose the category that best matches your question: general feedback, bug report, feature request, account support or business enquiry. The form asks for your name, email address, contact number and message so the support team has a way to respond.

What to include

For a conversion issue, tell us which tool you used, the type and approximate size of the file, the steps you followed and the exact error message. You can include the public page URL. Do not attach confidential files or include passwords, one-time codes, payment-card numbers or other sensitive information in the message.

Account and payment questions

For sign-in problems, describe the step that failed without sharing your password or OTP. For a payment question, include the payment or order reference and the date, but never send card credentials or banking passwords.

Privacy and data requests

Use the same form for privacy questions or a request about information you previously submitted. Include enough context to identify the request, but avoid sharing more personal information than needed. See the Privacy Policy for information about how support form submissions are handled.

Contact details

This website currently publishes the support form as its contact channel. We do not list a public street address or direct support email address here.
