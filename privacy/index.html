Privacy Policy

Your files belong to you. We build around that.

Effective date: 8 November 2026

File processing

When you use our converter tools, the files you select are processed locally on your device using browser technologies. File contents are not sent to our conversion server. Downloaded results are created in your browser; we do not retain copies of the files you select.

Information automatically handled by hosting

Our hosting provider may process standard technical requests such as IP address, user agent and timestamps for security, operation and diagnostics. This is separate from the content of any local files being converted.

Advertising and cookies

This site may use Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure performance, subject to relevant law and consent requirements. Review Google's advertising technologies and Google's privacy policy.

Third-party code

Our PDF processing libraries are bundled with the site. Google advertising scripts are loaded separately and may communicate with Google's services. We do not intentionally share selected file contents with Google Ads.

Data retention

We do not store uploaded conversion files. Optional accounts are managed by Supabase Auth, which processes email addresses, mobile phone numbers, authentication and profile metadata. Phone OTP uses an SMS delivery provider, which may receive the phone number for sending a one-time code. For paid members, the service stores membership dates, payment order identifiers and limited payment history. Razorpay handles payment details; we do not collect card numbers. Browser memory and temporary conversion download URLs are cleared as you leave or refresh the page.

Sign in and payments

Account logins may involve Supabase email OTP, SMS OTP, passwords, Google or GitHub. When paid checkout is activated, Razorpay processes your payment and returns limited confirmation data to our server to activate access. We do not store card details. Account sessions can be kept in local browser storage to keep you signed in. SMS may be subject to messaging fees and abuse-prevention checks. Contact the operator using the published contact channel to request support or account data requests.

Your options

You can configure cookie preferences in your browser and use applicable consent controls if they are offered. If you choose not to allow advertising cookies, personalized advertising may be limited.

Changes

We may update this policy if our services or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies this version.
