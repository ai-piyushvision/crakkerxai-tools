About CrakkerXworld.AI

A focused set of browser-based tools for everyday PDF and image tasks.

What CrakkerXworld.AI does

CrakkerXworld.AI is an online workspace for common PDF and image jobs. The site provides eight focused browser tools: Images to PDF, PDF to PNG, Merge PDF, Extract PDF Pages, Rotate PDF, Convert Image Format, Resize Image and Compress Image. Each tool has its own instructions, supported-format details, limits and frequently asked questions.

Who the tools are for

The tools can help students preparing notes, office teams organizing reports and scanned documents, administrators collecting forms, and creators preparing images for websites or sharing. They are designed for individual file tasks rather than a full document-management system.

How file processing works

The eight public converters use JavaScript libraries in the visitor's browser. Selected file contents are processed on the device and are not sent to the conversion server by the converter code. A combined input limit of 50 MB applies to a task, and browser memory, file compatibility and individual tool limits can affect results. Account, feedback, advertising and optional AI Updates features are separate services and are explained in the Privacy Policy.

Our product principles

We aim to keep each tool understandable: show the accepted formats, explain the available options, report errors and provide a direct download when processing succeeds. The converters do not add a watermark to output files. We encourage users to review converted files and keep originals of important documents.

How the service is supported

The core tools are available without an account. The site may display clearly labelled advertising, and it offers optional account and paid-access features. Advertising is separate from conversion controls; users should never need to click an ad to receive a file.

Contact and feedback

Questions, bug reports and feature requests can be sent through the Contact Us page or the Feedback & Bug Reports form.
