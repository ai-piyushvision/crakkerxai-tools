About CrakkerX AI

Little tools. Big time savers.

Why we built this toolkit

CrakkerX AI provides easy-to-use tools for everyday PDF and image tasks. Instead of asking you to create an account or upload personal files to a server, common editing and conversion tasks run directly in your browser.

What you can do

Combine images into a PDF, convert PDF pages to images, merge PDFs, extract or rotate pages, change picture formats, resize images and compress them for convenient sharing.

Our approach

We aim for clear tools, simple mobile-friendly controls and visible download links. We do not apply watermarks to your output. Browser capabilities and input formats have limits, so review your finished files before relying on them.

Keeping the tools free

We may display clearly identified advertising on this website. Ads are separate from download buttons, and you will never be required to click an ad to receive a converted file.
