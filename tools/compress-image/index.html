About Compress Image

Compress images using efficient JPG or WebP encoding, adjustable quality, and optional downscaling. Processing stays on your device.

Common questions

Will file size always shrink?

Not always. Already optimized images may remain similar in size.

Can I keep PNG transparency?

Choose WebP to retain transparency. JPG replaces transparent regions with white.
