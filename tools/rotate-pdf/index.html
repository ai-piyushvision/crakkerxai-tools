About Rotate PDF

Rotate all PDF pages or specific page numbers 90°, 180°, or 270° clockwise in your browser.

Common questions

Can I rotate only one page?

Yes. Enter that page number in the optional pages box.

Will this change the page content?

No. It updates page rotation metadata; the original content stays intact.
