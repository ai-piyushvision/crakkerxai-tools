Use this online image format converter to change JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images, including PNG to JPG or JPG to PNG conversions. Choose the output format and quality, process several pictures in one task, and download the converted images in a ZIP file.

SUPPORTED FORMATS

Input: JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP. Output: PNG, JPG or WebP. The quality control applies to lossy JPG and WebP encoding; PNG is exported without a lossy quality setting. Combined input is limited to 50 MB.

PRIVATE PROCESSING

Selected file contents are processed locally in your browser. This conversion tool does not upload your files to the conversion server.

INPUT SIZE

Up to 50 MB combined input per task. Browser memory and document complexity can affect processing time.

How to use Convert Image Format

  1. Choose one or more JPG, JPEG, PNG or WebP images.
  2. Select PNG, JPG or WebP as the output format and adjust quality when available.
  3. Click Convert Images. Download the converted image or the ZIP containing batch results.

Supported formats and options

Input: JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP. Output: PNG, JPG or WebP. The quality control applies to lossy JPG and WebP encoding; PNG is exported without a lossy quality setting. Combined input is limited to 50 MB.

When this tool is useful

  • Convert PNG screenshots to JPG for smaller, widely compatible files.
  • Convert JPG or PNG images to WebP for supported websites and apps.
  • Convert several images to a common format and download them together.

A practical example

Convert a group of PNG screenshots to WebP for a website, or export a transparent PNG to JPG when a white background is acceptable. Multiple results are downloaded together as a ZIP.

Limitations to know before converting

Inputs are JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP; outputs are PNG, JPG or WebP. JPG cannot keep transparent pixels, which are filled white. The quality setting affects lossy JPG/WebP output, not PNG. Very large images can hit the 30-megapixel canvas limit or browser memory limits.
